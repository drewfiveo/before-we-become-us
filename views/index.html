01 The idea

Becoming comes before belonging.

Every relationship we build is influenced by the person we bring into it.

Before We Become Us exists to create a place where people can pause, learn, ask questions, find guidance and develop the understanding they need to build healthier lives and relationships.

We bring together people, professionals, knowledge and organizations so that meaningful support is easier to discover.
